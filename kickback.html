Reloaded
Reloaded is a Multiplayer FPS in Unity using Photon PUN 2. In Reloaded, the rifle is completely manual. Players must complete a string of button prompts, shown on screen, to complete steps in the reload animation. This, along with a tight map and fast movement, means a high stakes environment where every shot counts and accuracy is key to victory.

Development
Kickback uses a custom procedurally generated level built from the ground up. It uses modules that are randomly picked and spawned in a line from the start block, with checkpoints that clear previous levels. WRITE MORE
Reception
Players don't notice that the game is procedurally generated. While this is partly because of a lack of modules in its current state, this is actually a good thing. It's only on their second playthrough that players realize that the entire level is building in front of them. Players love the movement mechanics of the guns, especially the shotgun.

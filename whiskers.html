General Whiskers: Armored Avenger
Built as the final project for Game Engines at the University of Montana, General Whiskers: Armored Avenger (GW:AA) is a top-down tank game where you take control of General Whiskers as he chases down the cat who stole his grandchildren.

Development
Originally a group project, GW:AA became a solo work early in development. Building in Unreal Engine 5 with blueprints, it was quickly obvious that there was nothing built in to handle camera movement. The game has a top-down camera that only moves in between levels, and is otherwise stationary over the whole level. This had to be hand created using a data table with position values. The checkpoint between levels tells the camera to move, and it looks for the next row in the table for a location value. During development, I found I could add dev keybinds, allowing me to move the camera manually, allowing streamlined level testing.

Speaking of level testing, the level design of this game started with input from my partner in the group. Through testing, it was obvious that reworking of these designs was needed. I coordinated with playtesters to tweak these levels, leaving them challenging, but fair.

The player and enemy models were all modeled and painted by myself. Some environment models were found online, while others were pieced together in Unreal with basic shapes.

Reloaded
Reloaded is a Multiplayer FPS in Unity using Photon PUN 2. In Reloaded, the rifle is completely manual. Players must complete a string of button prompts, shown on screen, to complete steps in the reload animation. This, along with a tight map and fast movement, means a high stakes environment where every shot counts and accuracy is key to victory.

Idea
The idea for this game came to me a few years ago. Theres so much skill in aiming and movement in flat screen games. However, in VR games, like Pavlov VR, reloading is a part of the skills required to win. I wanted to bring this to flat screen FPS games. The result is intense moments, where a player with great movement and aim struggles to chamber the next round, giving their opponenet a moment to strike.

Reception
In early playtesting, players have found the game fun, especially after the implementation of a map. The high stakes and fast gameplay keeps them engaged, and the skill expression leads to hypercompetitivity.
